About SHADOWFLIX

SHADOWFLIX helps you discover movies, explore genres, read descriptions, check ratings and find trailers. It is a movie discovery website, not a streaming service.

Movie information and images are provided by TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
